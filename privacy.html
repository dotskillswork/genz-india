Privacy Policy for Gen'Z India

Last updated: August 2, 2026

At Gen'Z India, accessible from our platform, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our students and visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Gen'Z India and how we utilize it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign up or log in, we collect basic details such as your username, display name, and profile display info.
  • User Content & Interactions: Any messages, doubts, community chats, notes, or uploaded study materials you share within the platform are stored securely.
  • Learning & Assessment Data: Data related to your progress in skill courses, AI study buddy chats, mock interview transcripts, and sample test scores are recorded to provide personalized feedback and certification tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our learning platform and certificate generators.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our AI features, test series, and skill courses.
  • Understand and analyze student engagement and platform usage trends.
  • Communicate with you for customer support, platform updates, and notifications.

3. Data Security & Storage

We use secure enterprise cloud services (such as Firebase) to store user data safely. However, please note that no method of electronic transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services & AI Processing

Parts of our learning hub utilize advanced AI models and cloud APIs for features like AI Study Buddy and Mock Interviews. User prompts and voice interactions may be processed to return accurate academic and interview feedback, adhering to strict data confidentiality standards.
